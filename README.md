# ecommerce-sales-analysis
E-commerce sales analysis using SQL and Excel, with an interactive dashboard and business insights.

## Project Overview
Analysis of 5,000 e-commerce transactions using MySQL and Excel to evaluate sales, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance, through an interactive Excel dashboard and a detailed business report. The analysis aims to support inventory planning, pricing, and marketing decisions.

## Data used
- <a href="https://github.com/AKRAMHOUSSA/ecommerce-sales-analysis/blob/main/Ecommerce_Sales_Data_2024_2025.csv">Dataset_sales

## Business Questions
- Which product generated the highest revenue? 
- How loyal are the store's customers based on purchasing behavior? 
- Which region and city generated the highest revenue and sales quantity? 
- Which category and sub-category generated the highest sales and profit? 
- Which payment mode is most frequently used by customers? 
- How do monthly sales and profit change over time? 
- Is there a relationship between discount and sales revenue across sub-categories?

## Key Findings
- Home Decor generated the highest revenue, while Furniture achieved the strongest overall profitability.
- Sofa was the top-performing sub-category in revenue, profit, and sales quantity.
- Customer purchasing behavior was highly concentrated, with 96.82% of customers making three purchases.
- North was the strongest-performing region, while Bangalore generated the highest city revenue.
- 2024 was the strongest-performing year.
- Higher discounts showed only a weak positive relationship with sales revenue.
- October 2025 recorded unusually low transaction activity and should be further investigated.

## Recommendations
- Strengthen high-performing categories, especially Furniture and Sofa.
- Increase stock allocation for regionally preferred products — Orange Juice in Los Angeles and Notebook in New York — based on localized demand patterns.
- Investigate low performance of Apple through pricing, placement, or customer feedback review, rather than assuming a demand or quality issue from sales data alone.
- Consider a loyalty promotion tied to Shampoo purchases, since it's already the strongest reward-point driver among members.
